# ⚡ SOLO LEVELING: MULTIPLAYER SQUAD DRAFT & BATTLE ARENA

A turn-based multiplayer card draft and PvP tournament battle game set strictly within the canon **Solo Leveling universe**, accurately scaled according to author lore and canonical power hierarchies.

---

## 🎮 Core Features & Architecture

* **👑 34 Canon Solo Leveling Legends**: Grounded in canon power scaling, feats, abilities, and 7-role drafting eligibility.
* **🛡️ Strict No-Repetition Rule**: Once a card is drawn and assigned or discarded by any player, it can never appear again for any other player during the match.
* **👥 1-8 Player Setup & Guild Customizer**: Play Solo Dungeon mode, 1v1 Hunter Duel, 3P Tri-Guild Showdown, 4P Grand Raid, or up to 8 Players. Customize guild names and order.
* **✏️ Hunter Roster Manager & Editor**: Search, filter by tier, customize power levels and stats, add custom hunter cards, or reset to master canon defaults.
* **🎨 4 Background Atmospheric Themes**:
  - 🌑 **Shadow Monarch** (Jinwoo Void Realm / Neon Blue & Monarch Violet)
  - 👑 **Ruler's Light** (National Hunter Gold & Celestial Azure)
  - ⚔️ **Red Gate / Demon Castle** (Crimson Inferno & Dark Slate)
  - ❄️ **Frost Gate** (Glacial Deep & Ice Cyan)
* **🔊 100% Offline Web Audio Synthesizer**: Built-in sound effects (Card draws, Monarch aura hums, combat clashes, fanfare) requiring zero external audio downloads.
* **⚔️ Animated Squad Battle Arena**: Dynamic matchup simulation featuring HP bars, tactical combat round logs (Vanguard initiative, Mage artillery, Assassin flanks, Healer clutch restorations, Monarch ultimate finishers), and victory celebrations with confetti.

## 👥 The 7 Squad Positions

Every completed squad must fill:
1. 👑 **TEAM LEADER**
2. ⚔️ **FIGHTER**

4. 🛡️ **TANK**
5. 💚 **HEALER** *(Min Byung-Gyu, Lee Joohee, Akari Shimizu, Han Semi, Jung Yerim)*

### Synergies & Multipliers:
* **Weighted Power Formula**: $\text{Raw Power (30\%)} + \text{Hax (20\%)} + \text{Speed (15\%)} + \text{Durability (10\%)} + \text{Synergy (15\%)} + \text{Battle IQ (10\%)}$
* **Role Match Bonus**: $+15\%$ to character combat power when placed in their canonical eligible role.
* **Monarch's Dominion**: True Shadow Monarch / God Tier Leader $\rightarrow$ **+10% Total Squad Combat Power**.
