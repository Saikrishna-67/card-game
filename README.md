## 👥 Multiplayer & PvP Battle Arena Mechanics

### 1. Multiplayer Turn-Based Drafting
* Select **2 Players (1v1 PvP)**, **3 Players**, or **4 Players (Tournament)**.
* Players take turns drawing random character cards with **`.`** (period) key or the **DRAW CARD** button.
* On their turn, each player chooses to **KEEP** (assigning to one of their 7 slots) or **DISCARD** (permanently removes the card from the draft).
* Switch between player squad views at any time using the player tabs.

### 2. The 7 Squad Positions
Every completed squad must fill:
1. 👑 **TEAM LEADER**
2. ⚔️ **FIGHTER**
3. 🔮 **MAGE**
4. 🛡️ **TANK**
5. 💚 **HEALER** *(Min Byung-Gyu, Lee Joohee, Akari Shimizu, Han Semi, Jung Yerim)*
6. 💚 **SUPPORT**
7. 🗡️ **ASSASSIN**

### 3. Sovereign Dimensional Arena Simulation
When all players fill their 7 positions, the tournament starts:
* **Round 1: 👑 Leader vs Leader Sovereign Clash** (Aura supremacy & army command)
* **Round 2: 🔮 Mage & Artillery Bombardment** (Area spells, curses vs elemental storms)
* **Round 3: 🗡️ Assassin Infiltration Duel** (Stealth maneuvers & supersonic lethal strikes)
* **Round 4: 🛡️ Vanguard & Healer Sustain War** (Kinetic barriers & cellular regeneration)
* **Round 5: ⚡ All-Out Sovereign Clash** (Total team combat power, Hax multipliers, synergy buffs, and decisive victor announcement!)
